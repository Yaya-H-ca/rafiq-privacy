Privacy Policy — "Rafiq — My Quran Companion"

— Last updated: November 2026
Developer: Yaya H — Contact: ventouse.ca@gmail.com

Rafiq is a free app that does not collect any personal data.

  • No accounts, no servers: the app never asks you to create an account and never sends any information to any server.
  • Your data stays on your device: your reading position, wird log, memorization progress, favorites and settings are stored only locally on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Location (optional): the app accesses your location only if you tap the "Use my current location" button yourself. Coordinates are used on your device only, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and are kept on your device as your chosen location source. They are never transmitted to anyone and are never used for tracking.
  • No ads, no tracking: the app contains no advertising network and no analytics or tracking tools, and shares no data with third parties.
  • Backups: a backup file is created only when you request it in Settings; you save or share it yourself. The developer never has access to it.
  • Notifications: all alerts are local (prayer times, wird, adhkar) and are generated on your device.
  • Children: this is a religious educational app suitable for all ages; it collects no data from any user, child or adult.
  • Changes: any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date above.
